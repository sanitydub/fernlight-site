How an app gets made

The same four steps for every Fernlight app.

  1. Find the problem

    Start with something people deal with every day.

  2. Design it properly

    Shape the screens and steps until they feel simple.

  3. Build and launch

    Ship it once it works well and feels right.

  4. Keep improving

    Listen to the people using it, and keep refining.
